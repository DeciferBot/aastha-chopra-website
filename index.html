About Me

Curating the art of elevated living

I'm Aastha — a luxury lifestyle and fashion creator based in Dubai, passionate about curating beauty in the everyday. From considered style to intentional wellness, my world is one of aesthetic refinement and authentic expression.

Through my lens, I share a life thoughtfully composed — where fashion meets feeling, and wellness is a ritual worth celebrating. I believe that luxury isn't excess; it's the quiet elevation of all that surrounds you.
