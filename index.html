About Me

Curating the art of elevated living

I'm Aastha Chopra, a Dubai-based luxury lifestyle and fashion creator with 51.5K followers across the UAE and globally. Trained as a lawyer and now a UAE Media Council licensed creator, I bring the same precision and intentionality to my content as I did to the courtroom. My world spans designer fashion, premium beauty, and elevated living.

I work with the brands I genuinely love. From Valentino and Celine to Huda Beauty and Dolce & Gabbana, every piece of content I create is crafted to perform, not just to look good. My audience trusts my taste, and that trust is the most valuable thing I offer any brand partner.
