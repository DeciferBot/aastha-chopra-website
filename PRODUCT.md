# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Brand marketing managers, talent/influencer agencies, and PR teams (primarily UAE/Dubai and global lifestyle brands) evaluating Aastha Chopra (@aastha_sochic, ~51.5k IG followers) for paid collaborations. They arrive from Instagram bio links, outreach replies, or search, usually on mobile, scanning quickly to judge fit, reach, and professionalism before deciding to request a media pack or rates. Secondary audience: Aastha herself, who manages the site mobile-only.

## Product Purpose

Official website + media pack for a Dubai-based fashion/beauty/lifestyle creator. It exists to convert brand interest into a collaboration conversation: showcase persona and aesthetic, prove reach with real audience stats, present niche-specific landing pages (fashion, luxury, wellness), and route qualified leads to WhatsApp/email/media-pack. Success = booked collaborations and qualified inbound, not pageviews. A growing SEO "Journal" supports discovery.

## Brand Personality

Polished, aspirational, warm-luxury. Dubai glamour with approachable confidence. Three words: elegant, confident, inviting. The interface should evoke trust and premium quality (a brand should feel safe spending budget here) while staying personable, not cold or corporate.

## Anti-references

Not a generic Linktree or template influencer card. Not a SaaS landing page (no hero-metric grids, no gradient-text, no eyebrow-on-every-section scaffolding). Not cold luxury minimalism that reads as sterile. Not cluttered, banner-heavy creator pages that feel cheap.

## Design Principles

- **Proof over claims**: real follower/engagement numbers and real content beat adjectives.
- **One clear next action per surface**: every page funnels to collaborate / media pack.
- **Premium but personable**: luxury polish without losing the human behind the account.
- **Mobile-first reality**: the primary visitor and the owner are both on phones.
- **Niche clarity**: fashion / luxury / wellness pages each speak to that vertical's brands specifically.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (watch muted gold/gray on light backgrounds), visible focus states, meaningful alt text on portfolio imagery, reduced-motion alternatives for any scroll/entrance animation. Touch targets ≥44px given mobile-dominant traffic.
